Role accounts

Reusable device or bastion logins, not application permission roles. Updating an account changes credentials used by every profile referencing it.

    New role account

    Choose one authentication method. Leave its secret blank to preserve it when editing the same method. Changing methods requires a new credential and clears the old one. Keys and passwords are never displayed after saving. Referenced accounts cannot be deleted. Use only fake demo credentials in this local demonstration.
